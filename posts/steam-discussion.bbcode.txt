TITLE: [Linux / Steam Deck] Working fix for Trove not launching since XIGNCODE3

[h1]Trove on Steam Deck / Linux: working again[/h1]

If pressing [b]Play[/b] in Glyph does nothing since the XIGNCODE3 anti-cheat update, here's a fix that works on Steam Deck (tested November 2026).

[i]Disclosure: this fix, the setup script and this guide were made by an AI assistant (Claude) troubleshooting on my Steam Deck. I tested it and I'm sharing it so others can play again.[/i]

[h2]What's wrong[/h2]
The anti-cheat loader quits under [b]every Proton version[/b]. Ten were tested, including Proton Experimental, Hotfix and GE-Proton. Under [b]plain WineHQ Wine[/b] it starts normally and the game runs.

[h2]How to fix it[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Install Trove on Steam as usual and let it download. No need to launch it.
[*]Download the setup script: https://github.com/Catanic/trove-linux-fix
[*]Close Steam. In Desktop Mode, open a terminal in the script's folder and run:
[code]./install.sh --controller --add-to-steam[/code]
[*]Start Steam again and launch [b]Trove (Wine)[/b] from your library (Gaming Mode works).
[*]Log in to Glyph with your [b]Trove email and password[/b], then press Play.
[/olist]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Steam login doesn't work[/b] in this setup (it needs Proton). It's the same account; if you never set a password, use "Can't log in?" in Glyph.
[*]Steam Deck: press [b]View (⧉)[/b] in game to switch to controller prompts. Chat: click the chat box with the trackpad, then Steam + X for the keyboard.
[*]If Glyph gets stuck: run [i]~/Games/Trove-wine/trove.sh --restart[/i]
[*][b]Unofficial.[/b] The anti-cheat runs normally and isn't modified, but nobody can guarantee anything about bans. Use it at your own discretion.
[*]Tested on one Steam Deck so far. Please read the script before running it.
[/list]

[h2]Technical details[/h2]
The XIGNCODE loader checks [i]ntoskrnl.exe[/i] against Windows' signature catalog; under Proton that fails and it exits with code 1048. Plain Wine 11.0 doesn't hit that check. Details are posted for Valve on GitHub: ValveSoftware/Proton issue #2986.

If something doesn't work, post your device and what happens when you press Play.
